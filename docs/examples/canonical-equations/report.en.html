Lectures on differential equations for Feynman integrals ↗

Date: 2014-12-06 · Version: arXiv:1412.2296v1 (v1 checked directly)

The notes choose a three-element basis for the massless one-loop on-shell box family and obtain ∂x g = epsilon[a/x+b/(1+x)]g with constant matrices a and b.
